Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: Route2Charge has no user accounts, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not receive or store any personal data about you. To work, the app uses Apple Maps (routes and place search) and Open Charge Map (charging station data).

1. Who is responsible

Clemens Schweder
E-mail: route2charge@proton.me

2. The Route2Charge app

Location

If you start a route from your current location, the app asks for permission to use your location while you use the app. Your location is used on your iPhone to start the route and is passed to Apple Maps for routing. It is not sent to us and not stored by the app. You can also choose a different starting point and decline location access.

Routes and place search (Apple Maps)

Search terms, start, destination and the positions of suggested charging stations are processed by Apple Maps to search places and calculate routes and detours. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Charging station data (Open Charge Map)

To find charging stations, the app requests data for small rectangular map areas around your charging window from Open Charge Map. Your start, destination or full route are not transmitted, and no account or device identifier is used. As with any internet request, your IP address is technically transmitted to the provider’s servers. Information: openchargemap.org.

Settings

Your settings (for example battery size, consumption, minimum charging power) are stored only locally on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide anonymised crash reports to the developer; this is controlled entirely in your iOS settings.

3. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure. Data may be processed in the USA. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The website itself uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no JavaScript and no external fonts or third-party content.

If you contact us by e-mail, we use your message only to answer it and delete it when it is no longer needed. E-mails are received via Proton Mail (Proton AG, Switzerland), which stores them encrypted. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

An overview of every service and tool involved – and which data each one sees – is on the page Technology & legal.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, simply send an e-mail. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

5. Changes

If the app or this website changes in a way that affects your data, this policy will be updated accordingly.
