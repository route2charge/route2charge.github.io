Technology & legal

Everything Route2Charge is built with and runs on – which company is behind each part, what it does and which data it sees. Plus licences, disclaimer and trademarks.

In short: Route2Charge is an independent project by Clemens Schweder. The app has no server of its own, no account and no tracking. It talks to exactly two services: Apple Maps and Open Charge Map. This website is plain HTML and CSS on GitHub Pages – no JavaScript, no cookies.

Who is behind Route2Charge

Developer and contact: Clemens Schweder, e-mail route2charge@proton.me. Route2Charge is not affiliated with Apple, Tesla, Open Charge Map or any charging network.

The app

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Written in Swift.

Swift & SwiftUI Apple

Programming language and user interface.

Data: Runs on your iPhone only – no data leaves the device.

Apple Maps (MapKit) Apple Inc.

Map, place search, routes and detour calculation.

Data: Search terms, start, destination and the positions of suggested chargers are sent to Apple. Apple privacy policy

Open Charge Map API Open Charge Map (community project)

Charging station data: location, power, plugs, operator.

Data: Only small rectangular map areas around your charging window and the minimum power. Never your start, destination or route; no device ID. Your IP address is technically transmitted. openchargemap.org

Core Location Apple

Your current location – only if you start “from here”, once per search, and to notice when you have arrived at the charger.

Data: Stays on your iPhone; passed to Apple Maps for routing. Never stored, never sent to us.

UserDefaults (iOS) Apple

Stores your settings such as battery size or minimum power.

Data: Only on your iPhone. Deleted together with the app.

Mail app (iOS) Apple or your mail provider

Feedback – only when you tap “Report a bug”.

Data: Whatever you write; app and iOS version are visible in the message and can be deleted before sending.

Not included: analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising, tracking, third-party libraries or a server of our own.

This website

GitHub Pages GitHub, Inc. (USA)

Hosting of this website.

Data: Your IP address and technical request data are processed by GitHub to deliver the pages. GitHub Privacy Statement

HTML & CSS Built in-house

The pages are plain, static HTML and CSS, generated by a small Python script.

Data: No JavaScript, no cookies, no external fonts, no third-party content. A Content Security Policy blocks scripts and foreign content.

The website’s source code is public: github.com/route2charge/route2charge.github.io

Contact by e-mail

Data: Your e-mail address and message. Used only to reply and deleted when no longer needed. Proton privacy policy
