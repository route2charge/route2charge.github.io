Frequently asked questions

Where does the charging station data come from?

From Open Charge Map, a free community database, and its data providers. If a charger is missing, closed or shows the wrong power, you can correct it directly at openchargemap.org – or simply tell us.

Why does the app show prices?

It doesn’t yet. There is no free, open database for charging prices. We’re working on it.

Why does Apple Maps only navigate to the charger?

Apple Maps accepts only one destination from other apps. After charging, tap “Continue to destination” or “Find next charging stop from here” in Route2Charge.

Does the app need my location?

Only if you start from where you are, and to notice when you’ve arrived at the charger. You can also enter any starting point. Your location is never stored.

Which cars does it work with?

Any electric car with a CCS fast-charging plug – that’s almost every EV in Europe. For battery percentage mode, enter your battery size and average consumption in the settings.

Can I hide Tesla Superchargers?

Yes – in the settings, switch on “Hide Tesla Superchargers”.
